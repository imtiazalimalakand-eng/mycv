About Me

I am a dedicated plant science researcher and educator from Malakand, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. My M.Phil research at Hazara University — completed with a CGPA of 3.50/4.00 — focused on the biological control potential of Quercus incana Roxb. against Fusarium wilt of tomato, encompassing pathogen isolation, greenhouse experiments, microscopy, and PCR-based detection.

My scientific expertise spans plant pathology, applied mycology, ethnobotany, pharmacognosy, molecular biology, and experimental plant physiology. I have hands-on experience designing and executing antimicrobial assays, brassinosteroid stress trials, and regional floristic surveys across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Alongside research, I hold an M.Ed from Allama Iqbal Open University and currently serve as a Primary School Teacher under the District Government of KP, channelling scientific thinking into the next generation. I remain actively committed to advancing plant science research and am seeking a Ph.D. position at a leading research university.

Scientific Work

Research Experience

01

Plant Pathology & Microbial Ecology

M.Phil research centred on Fusarium wilt disease of tomato — one of the most destructive soilborne diseases globally — with a focus on natural biocontrol using plant extracts.

  • Isolation and characterisation of Fusarium spp.
  • Greenhouse cultivation and inoculation bioassays
  • Microscopy and PCR-based pathogen detection
  • Soil microbiology and pathogen ecology
  • Statistical design and analysis of experimental trials
02

Plant Physiology & Stress Biology

Investigated physiological responses of tomato to brassinosteroid supplementation and salt stress, with findings published in Plant Science Today.

  • Exogenous and foliar brassinosteroid applications
  • Salt stress experiments on Lycopersicon esculentum
  • Growth and physiological parameter analysis
  • Published: Plant Science Today, 4(3), 2017
03

Ethnobotany & Medicinal Plants

Field surveys and literature-based documentation of medicinal and economic plants of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, contributing to national plant resource knowledge.

  • Floristic checklist of Datta, District Mansehra
  • Medicinal plants survey of District Lower Dir
  • Traditional uses, pharmacological relevance, conservation
  • Herbarium specimen preparation and curation
04

Antimicrobial & Antifungal Research

Evaluated the bioactive potential of Quercus incana and Clematis graveolens against pathogenic bacteria and fungi using established assay protocols.

  • Ethanolic and aqueous plant extract preparation
  • Disk diffusion antibacterial assays
  • Antibiotic synergism and combination testing
  • Published: Middle-East J. Sci. Res., 24(3), 2016
